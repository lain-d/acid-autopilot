README

Every track is written on the spot in the genres you picked and blended into the next with a crossfade and a bass swap. With Camelot moves on, each new track stays in the same key or steps to a neighbouring key on the Camelot wheel (a fifth up or down), shown in green on the display. The knobs nudge whatever is playing: drag up or down, double-tap to reset. Lead amount: Some plays a hook at the peaks of most tracks (fewer in techno and hard techno), Lots adds a variation and uses it on every track, None mutes it. Sounds set to Auto follow each track. Genre, length, key and tempo settings take effect on the next track. No sound on an iPhone? Turn the silent switch off.
